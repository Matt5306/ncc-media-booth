Media team booth · three iPads · about 5 minutes each

iPad setup card. One trip into Settings, one scan, no typing.

Straight answer: an iPad has no one-click setup. Apple does not let a file, a link or a robot change Settings; the only real automation is Apple's management tools, and those wipe the iPad first. So this is the shortest route by hand. Do all three iPads side by side, one step at a time, like an assembly line. It is much faster than finishing one before starting the next.

Step 1. Settings, one trip Open the grey gear once and do all five before you leave it.

  1. Wi-Fi: join the Wi-Fi where you are. (Tonight in the gym: join the gym's.)
  2. Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never. Brightness slider most of the way up.
  3. General › Date & Time › Set Automatically: on.
  4. Safari › Camera › Allow. (Scroll down in the Safari settings to find Camera.)
  5. Accessibility › Guided Access: turn it on. Then Passcode Settings › Set Guided Access Passcode. Same number on all three. Write it on tape on the back.

Step 2. Scan this with the iPad's camera Open the Camera app, point it at the code, tap the yellow link that pops up. The booth opens in Safari.

Opens the booth Same as typing tinyurl.com/ncc-booth. Show this on your phone or a laptop screen and scan it from each iPad.
  1. When "Can you run a Sunday production?" shows, tap the Share button (square with an up arrow, top right).
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Go to the home screen. Open the new dove icon, Media Booth. Always use that icon from now on.

Step 3. Say yes to the camera, once

  1. In Media Booth tap Camera, then Play. When it asks to use the camera, tap Allow. You should see yourself.
  2. Go back to the list of games.

Step 4. Test the lock

  1. With Media Booth open, triple-click the top button (the round Home button on an older iPad). Tap Start.
  2. Triple-click again, type the passcode, tap End. That is all Guided Access is.
What you can skip: on an iPad that has not been played on, the Host settings are already right: round length is 60 and "After a game" is ON by default. Only open Host › Clear all scores on an iPad people have tested on. You will clear all three tomorrow morning anyway.

Step 5. Play once, on one iPad only Not all three.

  1. One round each of Sound, Camera, ProPresenter and Lower Thirds. At the end of each: a score, then SIGN UP and PLAY ANOTHER GAME.
  2. Camera game: thumb on the picture and drag is the joystick; pinch zooms.

Then plug all three in to charge.

Tonight in the gym, per iPad One minute each.

  1. Settings › Wi-Fi: join the gym Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the Media Booth icon once and tap into a game, so it is loaded.
  3. Clamp it on the tripod, charger cable down the leg.

Tomorrow morning, per iPad Thirty seconds each.

  1. Open Media Booth › Host › Clear all scores on this device.
  2. Triple-click the top button › Start.
If it goes wrong: blank page means Wi-Fi, rejoin and reopen the icon. Camera game shows a drawn room instead of you: Settings › Safari › Camera › Allow, or open the booth in Safari instead of the icon. Screen went dark: Auto-Lock was not set to Never. More fixes: setup guide, part 9.
